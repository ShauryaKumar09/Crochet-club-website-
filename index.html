Why us?

Wayzata Crochet strives to create a fun, low-stress, and creative environment for aspiring and expert crocheters!

Crochet Club welcomes crocheters at all levels, whether you’re just starting or already advanced. We’ll learn the basics of how to crochet together, and you’ll create small projects to enjoy or share with friends and family.

Recent event

Asian Fair

We set up a booth at the Asian Fair with a table full of handmade plushies and a spin-the-wheel prize game. It was such a joy sharing our makes, meeting the community, and seeing everyone’s favorites find new homes.
