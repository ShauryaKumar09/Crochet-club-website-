Why us?

Wayzata Crochet strives to create a fun, low-stress, and creative environment for aspiring and expert crocheters alike.

We welcome crocheters at all levels, whether you’re just starting or already advanced. We’ll learn the basics together and make small projects for you to enjoy or share with friends and family.
